Information Storage and Retrieval (ISR) Formulas Reference
1. Precision@K & Recall@K

Precision@K = |Retrieved_K ∩ Relevant| / K

Recall@K = |Retrieved_K ∩ Relevant| / |Relevant|

Precision measures fraction of retrieved documents that are relevant. Recall measures fraction of relevant documents that were successfully retrieved.

2. F1-Score

F1 = 2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall. Balances accuracy and completeness, penalizing extreme trade-offs between precision and recall.

3. Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR)

MRR = (1 / |Q|) × ∑ (1 / rank_first_relevant)

Evaluates how quickly the first relevant document appears. If first relevant result is at rank 1, RR=1.0; at rank 2, RR=0.5; at rank 3, RR=0.33.

4. Mean Average Precision (MAP)

MAP = (1 / |Q|) × ∑ AveP(q)

Mean of the Average Precision across all queries. Heavily rewards ranking all relevant documents at the very top of the retrieved list.
